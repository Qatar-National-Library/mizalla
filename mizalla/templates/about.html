01 / VISIBILITY

The right people, the right view.

Roles are per project: the same person can run one project as Project Manager or Delegated PM, build another as a team member, and sit in on a third as a vendor. Team members and Delegated roles edit; clients, stakeholders, and vendors review.

02 / DELIVERY

Progress with context.

Tasks, weighted steps, owners, JIRA references, and due-date filters give project teams a useful delivery picture.

03 / DECISIONS

Meetings connected to work.

Record agendas, attendees, and notes; create follow-up tasks; open future meetings as prefilled Teams compose links.
